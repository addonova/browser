# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful and productive for
everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and constructive when discussing code and design.
- Assume good intent, but challenge technical claims with evidence.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Respect differing browser, platform, and tooling constraints.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and release direction.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or exclusionary comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of issues, reviews, or discussions.
- Sharing credentials, private keys, or other secrets.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
chat channels, and other spaces managed by the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
private security advisory or moderation channel. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when this policy is violated.
